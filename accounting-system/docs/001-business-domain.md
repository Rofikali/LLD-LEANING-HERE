# Business Domain

## Core Concepts

### Account

Represents a financial bucket.

Examples:

- Cash
- Bank
- Sales
- Salary Expense

### Journal Entry

Represents a financial event.

Example:

Sale of product.

### Ledger

History of postings for an account.

### Trial Balance

Summary of debit and credit balances.

### Profit & Loss

Measures profit over a period.

### Balance Sheet

Snapshot of company financial position.
